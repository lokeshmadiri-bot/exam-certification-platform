Exam instructions

Please read the rules carefully. You must agree before you can proceed to the camera check.

Stay on the exam tab

Switching tabs, minimising or resizing the window each count as a strike. After 3 strikes the exam ends automatically.

Camera & lighting

Sit facing a light source and avoid backlight. Keep your face fully in frame for the whole exam.

Raise hand if you need help

Use Raise Hand to signal the invigilator. It pauses proctoring and answering. You may raise it up to 5 times.

Refresh, back or closing = ended

Refreshing the page, pressing back or closing the tab submits your answers and ends the attempt. You cannot re-enter.
